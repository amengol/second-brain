# 900 Archive

> Completed, dormant, or reference-only material

---

Where things go when they stop being live — finished projects, dead leads, superseded plans.

**Archive rather than delete.** A closed project is a record of what you tried and why it ended; that context is worth having when something similar comes up.

Move a whole project folder here rather than picking it apart, and leave a line in the project's own note saying *why* it was archived and *what would bring it back*. "Archived because it was quiet" and "archived because the client went silent after we quoted too high" are very different notes to reread in a year.
