# 500 Knowledge

> Learning notes, concepts, and deep dives you wrote

---

Your own understanding, written down. Concept notes, deep dives, lessons from something that broke, explanations you worked out for yourself.

The value is in the writing, not the collecting — a note you wrote badly is worth more than an article you saved perfectly, because writing it is what produced the understanding.

Link generously. A knowledge note with no `[[wikilinks]]` is a dead end; the connections are most of the point.
