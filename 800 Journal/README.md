# 800 Journal

> Daily notes and reviews — the heartbeat of the vault

---

One note per day, named `YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Weekly reviews live in `Weekly Reviews/`.

**This is the most important folder in the vault**, and the one most likely to be skipped. The daily note is where priorities get stated in the morning and checked honestly in the evening — which is what makes accountability possible at all. Without it, every other note is a snapshot with no story connecting them.

The daily template has three blocks that matter:
- **Morning Setup** — how you feel, your top priorities, what needs attention
- **Progress Log** — what you worked on, what actually moved, what got stuck
- **Reflections** — the win, the lesson, what you'd do differently

Five minutes in the morning and five at night is the whole commitment. Missing a day is fine. Missing three weeks is how you end up unable to remember what you decided or why.
